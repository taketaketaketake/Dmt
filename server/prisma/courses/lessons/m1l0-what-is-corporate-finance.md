## Definition*

- Corporate finance is a subset of finance that is focused on how businesses fund their operations in order to maximize profits and minimize costs.
- It deals with the day-to-day operations of a business' cash flows as well as with long-term financing goals (e.g., bank loans, venture capital funding, exit strategy).
- In addition to capital investments, corporate finance is concerned with monitoring cash flows, accounting, preparing financial statements, and taxation.
- *Investopedia.com

## What does it mean for you?

- Understanding corporate finance means that you can more accurately diagnose your company’s health so that you can:
  - Better understand your company’s performance
  - Better understand the effectiveness of your company’s operations
  - Better identify areas needing improvement, overhaul, or elimination
  - Better understand how to position your company for a successful financing round
  - Better position your company to meet and outperform your competition
- It’s hard to play the game if you can’t keep score.
- Now let’s look at some tools to help you diagnose your company’s health.

## Tools for Diagnosing the Health of Your Business

- Like with your personal health, corporate health requires regular attention and diagnosis in order to maintain and optimize its condition.
- Having the right tools for diagnosis ensures that you are accurately identifying areas that are performing well and areas that require attention.
- When it comes to diagnosing corporate health, the three main tools are:
- Balance Sheet (Statement of Financial Condition)
- Profit and Loss Statement (Statement of Income and Expenses)
- Cash Flows Statement
- From the information contained in these three reports, ratios can be calculated that show the relationship between different parts of your business, and more accurate analysis can be made.
  - Quick Ratio, Profit Margin, Return on Assets, Return on Cash, Turnover Ratio, Return on Sales, Return on Marketing, ROI, etc.

## The Magic Three


To successfully grow your business, you must be able to read and accurately diagnose your company’s health on a regular basis.


## Why the Magic Three

- As of a specific date:
  - What the company owns (Assets)
  - Who the company owes and has first right to Assets (Liabilities – vendors and lenders)
  - Who owns the remainder (Equity – investors and founders)
- Movement of cash in and out of the company
- How you are spending the company’s cash
- How much is from financing
- How much is used in operations
- How much is spent on capital expenditure
- How well your company’s Assets and resources are working together to generate revenue and profit
- How efficient are your operations’ procedures and processes in production, generating sales, and fulfillment.
