## Structuring Your Company’s Equity Offering

> **In this Topic, you will learn:**

- The different classes of equity owners in a business
- How net profit and loss is allocated to the various equity owners
- Types and order of distribution to equity owners
- Benefits and disadvantages of offering equity to finance the company

## The different classes of equity owners in a business

Equity in business represents ownership of the company’s remaining assets after all vendors and debtors are paid.

The characteristics that define and determine the class of equity held and order of distribution include:

:::cards
- **Preferred Stock Shares** — first to receive dividend and other payments
- **Common Stock Shares** — last to receive dividends and other payments
- **General Partnership Units** — responsible for managing the company; bears the most risk
- **Limited Partnership Units** — no management responsibility; risk limited to capital invested
- **Pre- and Post-SAFE Agreements** (Simple Agreement for Future Equity) — right to purchase equity at a discount or cap on valuation
:::

## How net profit and loss is allocated to the various equity owners

- When there are different classes of owners, the allocation of net profit and loss is determined by the **allocation rights** contained in each class of owner agreement
- Each class of owner agreement is either standardized or customized
- Typically, common stock shareholders and general partners are allocated the **remainder** after preferred shareholders and limited partners

## Types and order of distribution to equity owners

- Distribution to equity owners can be made in the form of **cash or other assets** such as stock or inventory
- Like with net profit and loss, the **order of distribution** is determined by the class of owner agreements
- Typically, general partners and common shareholders are paid **after** preferred shareholders and limited partners

## Benefits and disadvantages of offering equity to finance the company

There are both benefits and disadvantages to financing your company through an equity offering:

:::compare
### Benefits
- There’s no need to pay it back right away
- You can gain a strategic partner to share knowledge and connections as well as the risk
- A reduced personal credit risk
### Disadvantages
- You give up some control
- You give up a percentage of ownership on future returns
- There is a possibility of some stockholders being disruptive to operations or opportunities
:::
